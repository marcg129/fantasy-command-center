What this beta does not know yet

Live Sleeper roster/injury status and trending activity are automatic. After a transaction, Sleeper's public roster feed can take a short time to reflect the change even when this page is force-refreshing its API requests. v2.12 adds player-only Trade Intelligence using relative roster strengths, positional needs, season-long player value, current keeper context, and mutually complementary roster construction. Trade proposals intentionally exclude the connected manager's current keepers and injury stashes from default outgoing packages. Future draft-pick valuation and league-specific keeper-rights transfer rules are not yet modeled, while preserving required K/DST protection, QB/TE redundancy penalties, lighter trend weighting, and Sleeper depth-chart role weighting. The app knows whether a player is rostered, but this beta does not reliably distinguish every league-specific moment when an unrostered player is an immediate free agent versus subject to waivers — confirm the transaction button in Sleeper before acting. v2.10 now adds an automated headline feed generated by GitHub Actions. News classification is intentionally conservative and uses publisher-provided headlines/descriptions rather than scraping full articles. Independent projections, route participation, and richer red-zone opportunity remain future layers. Prior-season usage is automatically down-weighted when it is used as a Week 1 baseline, and weather remains a modest adjustment rather than an automatic bench signal.
v2.12 weekly beta uses Aug. 29/30 half-PPR expert consensus for the top of the board, removes league keepers from the available-player market, and then personalizes recommendations to whichever manager connects: their keeper positions, drafted roster, traded picks, exact future-pick spacing, and close-pick leverage. League scoring remains tuned to this league: 10 teams, 2 FLEX, 0.5 PPR, 6-point passing TDs, -4 interceptions, and yardage bonuses. Sleeper syncing is read-only. Weekly mode now adds IR-aware roster management, redundant-position logic, required K/DST protection, role-weighted free-agent analysis, league-specific free-agent detection, Sleeper trending signals, a legal-lineup optimizer, waiver-claim planning, player-only trade intelligence, nflverse schedule/game-environment context, nflverse recent player stats and snap counts, Open-Meteo outdoor weather adjustments, transactions, roster risk review, and matchup context.
