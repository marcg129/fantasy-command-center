What this beta does not know yet

Live Sleeper roster/injury status and trending activity are automatic. The app knows whether a player is rostered, but this beta does not reliably distinguish every league-specific moment when an unrostered player is an immediate free agent versus subject to waivers — confirm the transaction button in Sleeper before acting. Independent projections, practice reports, weather, snap/route data, Vegas totals, and current web news are not yet continuously fed into this static page. Those are the next layers before Week 1.
v2.3 weekly beta uses Aug. 29/30 half-PPR expert consensus for the top of the board, removes league keepers from the available-player market, and then personalizes recommendations to whichever manager connects: their keeper positions, drafted roster, traded picks, exact future-pick spacing, and close-pick leverage. League scoring remains tuned to this league: 10 teams, 2 FLEX, 0.5 PPR, 6-point passing TDs, -4 interceptions, and yardage bonuses. Sleeper syncing is read-only. Weekly mode now adds league-specific free-agent detection, Sleeper trending signals, an Immediate Post-Draft Scan for undrafted steals, more aggressive-but-protected roster churn review, transactions, roster risk review, and matchup context.
